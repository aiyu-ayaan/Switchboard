# Switchboard Design System — MASTER

One product on two platforms: the Android app (Jetpack Compose, Material 3
Expressive) and the desktop app (Electron, React, Tailwind). Both take their
colour roles from the same generated Material 3 scheme, so a surface, a
container or a primary button is the same colour on the phone and the PC.
Structure follows Domus and Kinema; the expressive shapes and wavy progress
follow Findex.

## Colour

Seed **Signal Indigo** `#4F5BD5` (primary, chroma 56) with an **Amber**
tertiary from `#E8873A` (chroma 48). Indigo is calm for a utility that sits
in the tray all day; amber is the accent for codes, external monitors and
"now playing". Neither collides with Domus (green), Kinema (ember) or
Findex (teal).

- `scheme.json`: every M3 role, light and dark, generated by `gen2.mjs`
  (`@material/material-color-utilities`, TonalSpot with custom palettes).
- `css.cjs` turns it into `frontend/src/tokens.css` (RGB triplets for Tailwind
  alpha). Android `ui/theme/Color.kt` carries the same hex values.
- On Android 12+ dynamic colour (wallpaper) is on by default; the static scheme
  is the fallback and the desktop always uses it.
- Status green `on` (`#1B6D3A` / `#8BD8A0`) is the only colour outside the
  scheme and means "connected / online" only.

| Role | Dark | Light | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| primary | `#BDC2FF` | `#4954BA` | Filled buttons, sliders, active nav |
| primaryContainer | `#303AA1` | `#E0E0FF` | Hero card, selected nav pill, app tile |
| tertiaryContainer | `#713700` | `#FFDCC6` | Pairing code chips, DDC monitors, dev flavor |

## Type

Desktop: Inter Variable (bundled, offline). Android: the system Roboto Flex
through the M3 type scale, with emphasized styles for hero numbers.
Desktop scale: caption 12, label 13, body 14, title 16, headline 22, page 28,
display 44.

## Shape

Pills for every button (they square up to 16 px while pressed, the expressive
press). Cards 20–28 px. Icons sit on M3 expressive shapes — Cookie 9 for
primary things, Cookie 6 / Flower / Clover for secondary, Sunny for success and
"playing" (slowly rotating). The desktop draws the same shapes as SVG
(`lobedPath` in `components/ui.tsx`).

## Motion

Android: `MotionScheme.expressive()` springs everywhere, shape morphs on state
changes, `LoadingIndicator` and wavy progress. Desktop: decelerate-in
(`cubic-bezier(0.05,0.7,0.1,1)`, 450 ms) with ≤ 50 ms stagger, an overshoot
curve standing in for springs on switches and the success "pop". All motion
stops under reduced-motion.

## Layout

- Desktop: floating pill rail (72 px, widens to 232 px on hover, Domus), a
  44 px draggable title bar with the OS buttons overlaid, content max 1040 px.
- Android: large top app bar with the computer's name as a display headline,
  system switcher chips, cards in a single column; FAB menu to add a computer.

## Brand

`brand/logo-mark.svg` — **Switch-S**: the S is the track of a toggle switch
and the knob sits at its "on" end. One filled path on a 256 grid: a 52-unit
track with round ends, 34-unit bends, and the knob a hole of r18 at (178, 60).
`brand/logo-mark-small.svg` drops the knob for 16–24 px.

The language it seeds: thick rounded tracks and round knobs, the same pills as
the buttons. The track is the connection, the knob is its state; amber on the
knob means "on / connected".

App tile `brand/app-icon.svg`: `#303AA1` tile, `#E0E0FF` track, `#FFB784`
knob. `brand/app-icon-dev.svg` swaps to the amber tile with an indigo knob.
`brand/render.sh` renders the desktop PNGs; the Android launcher icon and the
splash are the same path as vector drawables. The splash plays the connection:
the knob rides the S from the phone end to the PC end, drawing the track
behind it, then switches on in amber with a ping.

## Never

Emoji as icons, gradients on controls, coloured text on coloured containers
outside their on-pair, a QR code on a dark background (scanners need dark on
white), status shown by colour alone.
